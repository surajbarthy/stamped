# Site style

Standing reference for the marketing page and the community wall. Check a visual change against the projects, not against the portfolio's own layout.

## Concepts

- **Making Time.** The object is the page. Show the stamp on a real page. Empty frames labeled Screenshot do not count.
- **The Listening Wall.** A first reaction, left for the next person, with no name. The wall is that room. Randomize reshuffles it.
- **Tint-o-mix.** A gesture lays a mark on a live picture, and the picture is what you keep. Explain that as one sequence.
- **Questions.** One geometry, one meaning. The ring is the judgment. It appears where a stamp lands, with the words inside it, and not as a repeated logo.
- **Everydays.** A running practice. Time under each picture is the caption.
- **Bot-thy.** A public post is a fragment. Picture and time only.

## Visual material

Taken from visual work only.

- Questions: a line system whose shape is the idea.
- Everydays and the ink drawings: white line, red as pigment inside a form, paper, concentric circles.
- Mookambika: a hard limit of materials. White, black, and one red, used as matter.

## Check before shipping a visual change

- Is the stamp shown doing its job on a real picture?
- Does the ring mean "this is marked," and is it used only there?
- Is the wall a sequence of reactions, with the time as the only caption?
- Did a new button, bar, or extra seal appear that does not carry the idea? Remove it.
