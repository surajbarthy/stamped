Wall rules

Updated October 6, 2026

Stamp content, not people. A stamp is someone's opinion that a page looks like AI slop. It is not proof that anything is fake.

What can go on the wall

  • Pages, posts, ads and images you think are AI slop.
  • Public companies, brands and public figures' public posts.

What can't

  • Photos of private people, or anyone's face you haven't covered.
  • Names, usernames, emails or anything else that points to a private person.
  • Posts meant to single someone out or pile on them.
  • Anything illegal, hateful or sexual.

Every post is checked by a person before it shows. We turn down anything that breaks these rules, and we can take any post down at any time, for any reason.

Is this you or your work?

If you're in a picture on the wall, or you made or own something in one, press Report on it or write to report@slopstamp.xyz with the picture's link. We'll take it down. You don't need to prove anything first.

Copyright

Pictures on the wall are screenshots posted by people using the extension, as commentary. If you hold the rights to something shown and want it removed, write to the same address. We remove it once we've confirmed which picture you mean.

The fine print

Posts are the opinions of the people who posted them, not ours. Slop Stamp is free and comes as it is, with no promise that the wall stays up or that any post stays on it. By posting, you confirm the picture follows these rules.

No accounts. No names. A picture and a time.
